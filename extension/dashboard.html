01 · BROWSER SHIELD

Install Harbor Guard

  1. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select this project’s extension folder.
  4. Pin Harbor Guard to your toolbar and reload open sites.

This is an unpacked development build. It is not yet published or independently security audited.

02 · PC PROTECTION

Let Windows handle malware

Open the Windows Start menu, search for Windows Security, then select Virus & threat protection → Quick scan.

This project also includes optional PowerShell helpers in the windows folder to check Defender status and request a quick scan.

The extension cannot inspect your files, scan downloads, or verify your antivirus status.
KNOW YOUR SHIELD

What protection covers

  • Bundled EasyList/EasyPrivacy domain filters and local entries.
  • Local rules applied by your browser, even when the dashboard is closed.
  • Recognized ad slots and a best-effort YouTube helper.
  • Controls for each layer, plus per-site exceptions.

Reading bundled filter details…

Filter updates are manual in this version. Some advertising, tracking, phishing, and malicious sites will not be blocked.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your data stays with you

No account, remote service, telemetry, or browsing history collection. Only your preferences, aggregate cleanup counters, and recent cleanup events are stored.

Website access lets the extension identify ad elements and apply your site exceptions. Keep your browser’s built-in phishing and malware protection enabled.
